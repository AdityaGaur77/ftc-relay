Questions

Common questions.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Relay is free for teams. Mentors volunteer their time, and the nonprofit covers the rest.

Who can be a mentor?

FTC alumni, students currently competing with at least one full season behind them, and engineers or judges with FTC experience. Every mentor profile shows their real name and team history.

Why are sessions recorded?

Two reasons. It gives parents and the nonprofit a record of every session, and it is how mentors prove their volunteer hours. Teams agree to recording before they can book.

Can a parent or coach join the session?

Yes. Parents and coaches are welcome on any session. Many teams join as a group from their build space.

How are mentors rated?

After a session, the team says whether it helped or didn't, with an optional comment. Only teams who booked that mentor can leave feedback, and the totals are shown on every profile.
