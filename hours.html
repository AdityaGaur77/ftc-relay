Volunteer hours.

Relay is run through our nonprofit, so time you spend mentoring can count as volunteer service. There's a cap, and we need proof, so please read the rules before you log anything.

The rules
  1. Every call is recorded.

    Hit record (Zoom, Meet, Discord, whatever) before you start. Tell the team at the top of the call that it's being recorded. They also agreed to it when they booked.

  2. Hours are capped.

    You can log up to hours per season, and one call counts for at most hours. That keeps it about mentoring, not farming hours.

  3. Log it within a week.

    Paste the recording link, the team you helped, and roughly what you covered. Make sure the link can be opened by anyone with it.

  4. We review, then approve.

    Someone from the NPO watches enough of the recording to confirm it happened and how long it ran. Then it shows as approved and counts toward your total.

  5. Board replies don't count.

    Only live, recorded sessions with a team. Helping on the board is great, it just isn't something we can verify as hours.

Recordings are only used to verify hours and are deleted at the end of the season. Mentors under 18 should check with a parent before recording calls.
